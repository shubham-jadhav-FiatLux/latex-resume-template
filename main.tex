    } \\ \hline
    \CellStrut \SideLabel[2\baselineskip]{Context \& Problem} & The Capacitated Vehicle Routing Problem (CVRP) is an \textbf{NP-hard combinatorial optimization problem} which asks for a set of \textbf{minimum-cost routes} that start and end at a single depot. The routes serve a set of customers with known demands, and every vehicle has the same limited capacity. Each customer must be visited exactly once, and the total demand on a route may not exceed the vehicle capacity. CVRP sits at the core of logistics, parcel delivery and distribution planning, where even small savings in travelled distance add up to large \textbf{reductions in cost and emissions}. \\ \hline
    \CellStrut \SideLabel[1.5\baselineskip]{\parbox{\LabelWidth}{Approach \& \\ Contributions}} & 
    \vspace{-1.5ex}
    \begin{Bullets}
      \item Developed a solver built from \textbf{simple, fast heuristics}: a geometry-aware construction, granular local search, and a ruin-and-recreate metaheuristic applied through a size-independent decomposition and recombination scheme with parallel execution.
      \item On \textbf{million-scale benchmark instances}, our solver \textbf{beats the state of the art} on average within a few seconds, and it improves best-known solutions within 600 seconds.
      \item Achieves roughly a \textbf{10$\times$ reduction in runtime} compared to SOTA, on a standard 8-core machine.
    \end{Bullets}
    \vspace{-1ex}
  \end{tabularx}
\end{ResumeSection}
